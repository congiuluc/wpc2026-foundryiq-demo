# Ven Systems Model 12 Product Manual

## Overview
The Ven Systems Model 12 is a high-capacity liquid coating assembly system designed for precision application of emulsion layers onto graphene-based smart mesh fabrics. It supports sheet feeds ranging from 24 to 64 inches in width and integrates advanced temperature and flow control mechanisms to ensure uniform coating thickness and material integrity.

## Role in Manufacturing
Applies liquid emulsion coatings to graphene mesh fabrics, preparing them for sensor integration and environmental resistance.

## Scenarios for Use
- Initial coating of raw graphene mesh sheets before sensor embedment.
- Re-coating during refurbishment cycles.
- Application of specialized chemical layers for environmental resistance.

## Operating Parameters and Constraints
### Parameters:
- **Sheet Width**: 24–64 inches
- **Operating Temperature**: 20°C to 80°C
- **Flow Rate**: 0.5 to 5.0 L/min
- **Voltage**: 220V AC
- **Humidity Tolerance**: 10% to 85% RH

### Constraints:
- Do not exceed maximum sheet width.
- Ensure emulsion viscosity is within specified range.
- Avoid operation in environments below 10°C.

## Safety Indications
- Wear chemical-resistant gloves and goggles.
- Ensure proper ventilation when using volatile emulsions.
- Disconnect power before maintenance.

## Operating Procedures
1. Power on system and allow temperature stabilization.
1. Load sheet feed and align rollers.
1. Select emulsion type and set flow rate.
1. Initiate coating cycle and monitor nozzle output.
1. Perform post-coating inspection and drying.

## Maintenance Guidelines
- Clean emulsion nozzles daily.
- Inspect rollers for wear weekly.
- Calibrate temperature sensors monthly.

## Troubleshooting Tips
- Uneven coating: Check nozzle alignment and flow rate.
- System overheating: Inspect temperature control unit.
- Sheet jam: Realign feed rollers and clear obstructions.
