# Graphene Vapor Reclaim Tank

## Overview
The Graphene Vapor Reclaim Tank is a specialized system designed to capture and recycle graphene vapor generated during coating and sublimation processes. It ensures environmental compliance and material efficiency in Zava’s production lines.

## Role in Manufacturing
This tank collects excess graphene vapor, condenses it, and filters it for reuse in subsequent manufacturing cycles, reducing waste and operational costs.

## Scenarios for Use
- Reclaiming vapor during liquid coating of smart fabrics.
- Recycling graphene from sublimation chambers.
- Filtering airborne particulates in nano sensor production.

## Operating Parameters and Constraints
- Operating Pressure: 1.2 to 2.5 bar
- Condensation Temperature: 5°C to 10°C
- Vapor Flow Rate: 10 to 50 L/min
- Power Supply: 208V, 3-phase
- Filter Replacement Interval: 30 days

## Safety Indications
- Wear respiratory protection during filter changes.
- Monitor pressure gauges to prevent over-pressurization.
- Ensure grounding of tank to avoid static discharge.
- Follow hazardous material handling protocols.

## Operating Procedures
1. Activate condensation coils and verify temperature.
2. Monitor vapor intake and adjust flow rate.
3. Inspect filter membranes for saturation.
4. Collect condensed graphene in storage vessel.
5. Log reclaim metrics and prepare for reuse.

## Maintenance Guidelines
- Daily: Check pressure valves and inspect coil integrity.
- Weekly: Replace filter membranes and clean intake ports.
- Monthly: Test reclaim efficiency and calibrate sensors.

## Troubleshooting Tips
- Low reclaim rate: Inspect condensation coils and verify flow rate.
- Pressure spikes: Check valve operation and inspect seals.
- Filter clogging: Replace membranes and clean housing.
