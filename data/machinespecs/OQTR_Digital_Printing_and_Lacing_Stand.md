
# OQTR Digital Printing and Lacing Stand

## Overview
The OQTR Digital Printing and Lacing Stand is a high-precision system used for printing conductive patterns and lacing smart mesh fabrics. It ensures accurate deposition of inks and alignment of lacing elements for optimal performance.

## Role in Manufacturing
- Printing conductive traces on graphene fabrics
- Lacing structural and sensor elements
- Preparing fabric for final assembly

## Scenarios for Use
- Production of sensor-embedded athletic gear
- Custom printing of logos and data circuits
- Integration of lacing for wearable electronics

## Operating Parameters and Constraints
- Ink viscosity range: 10–100 cP
- Print resolution: 600 dpi
- Lacing tension: 5–20 N
- Power: 110V, 60Hz
- Max fabric width: 48 inches

## Safety Indications
- Use ventilation when handling conductive inks
- Avoid contact with moving lacing actuators
- Wear protective eyewear during printing

## Operating Procedures
1. Load fabric and align with collation rollers
2. Select print pattern and ink type
3. Configure lacing parameters
4. Start printing and monitor ink deposition
5. Initiate lacing sequence and inspect output

## Maintenance Guidelines
- Clean print heads daily
- Replace ink cartridges weekly
- Inspect lacing actuators monthly
- Lubricate feed rollers quarterly

## Troubleshooting Tips
- Ink smearing: Check viscosity and print head alignment
- Lacing misalignment: Recalibrate actuators
- Feed jams: Inspect roller tension and tray alignment
