# Contoso Robotic Garment Cut/Sew Assembly System

## Overview
The Contoso Robotic Garment Cut/Sew Assembly System automates the cutting and stitching of smart mesh fabrics into finished garments. It integrates precision robotics with adaptive sewing technologies to support Zava’s global apparel production.

## Role in Manufacturing
This system transforms raw smart mesh sheets into wearable products by executing programmed cut patterns and stitching routines, ensuring consistency and scalability.

## Scenarios for Use
- Cutting and assembling athletic wear with embedded sensors.
- Producing industrial uniforms with integrated safety features.
- Customizing garments for professional sports teams.

## Operating Parameters and Constraints
- Cutting Speed: 100 to 300 mm/s
- Stitching Accuracy: ±0.1mm
- Fabric Thickness Range: 0.2mm to 2.0mm
- Power Supply: 220V, 50Hz
- Supported Materials: Graphene mesh, nylon blends, elastomers

## Safety Indications
- Keep hands clear of cutting blades during operation.
- Use protective eyewear when inspecting stitching modules.
- Ensure emergency stop is accessible and functional.
- Follow lockout/tagout procedures before servicing.

## Operating Procedures
1. Load fabric sheets onto feed trays.
2. Select garment pattern from control interface.
3. Initiate cutting sequence and monitor blade alignment.
4. Begin automated stitching and inspect seam quality.
5. Remove finished garments and perform quality checks.

## Maintenance Guidelines
- Daily: Sharpen cutting blades and clean sewing heads.
- Weekly: Lubricate actuators and inspect feed trays.
- Monthly: Calibrate stitching accuracy and update firmware.

## Troubleshooting Tips
- Blade misalignment: Recalibrate cutting module and inspect mounts.
- Stitching errors: Check thread tension and actuator response.
- Feed jams: Clear tray obstructions and verify fabric alignment.
