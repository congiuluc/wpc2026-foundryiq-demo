# Electro-card Strand Extrusion and Coupling Printer

## Overview
The Electro-card Strand Extrusion and Coupling Printer is engineered to produce conductive strands used in smart mesh fabrics. It combines high-precision extrusion with automated coupling mechanisms to ensure consistent electrical performance across Zava’s wearable technologies.

## Role in Manufacturing
This machine fabricates and couples electro-conductive strands that serve as the backbone for data transmission and power delivery in sensor-embedded garments.

## Scenarios for Use
- Producing conductive pathways for biometric sensors.
- Creating flexible circuits for motion tracking.
- Integrating power lines into industrial safety gear.

## Operating Parameters and Constraints
- Extrusion Temperature: 180°C to 220°C
- Strand Diameter Range: 0.1mm to 1.0mm
- Coupling Tolerance: ±0.05mm
- Power Supply: 240V, 60Hz
- Material Compatibility: Silver-plated nylon, graphene composites

## Safety Indications
- Use thermal gloves when accessing heated components.
- Ensure emergency stop is functional before operation.
- Avoid direct contact with extruded strands during cooling.
- Follow fire safety protocols for high-temperature zones.

## Operating Procedures
1. Load extrusion material into hopper.
2. Set temperature and diameter parameters.
3. Initiate strand extrusion and monitor flow rate.
4. Align coupling sensors and begin bonding sequence.
5. Inspect output strands for continuity and uniformity.

## Maintenance Guidelines
- Daily: Clean extrusion nozzles and check coupling sensors.
- Weekly: Inspect heating coils and replace worn components.
- Monthly: Calibrate alignment systems and test strand conductivity.

## Troubleshooting Tips
- Nozzle clogging: Use approved cleaning rods and solvents.
- Misalignment: Reconfigure sensor calibration and test feed rate.
- Strand breakage: Adjust tension settings and verify material quality.
